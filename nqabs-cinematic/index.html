About the Studio

Furniture built around your space, not the other way around.

Since 2010, NQABS Built-ins Services has designed and hand-fitted joinery for homes across the region: kitchens, wardrobes, media units, and everything in between. We work in solid timber and premium boards, cut and assembled to the exact dimensions of your room.

Every project starts with a conversation, not a catalogue. We measure twice, model the space in 3D, and build once, so what lands in your home is exactly what you pictured.
